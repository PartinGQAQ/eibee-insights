PLATFORM MECHANICS · 平台机制

不止一套算法：Feed、Reels、Stories 与推荐流的排序逻辑

Instagram 静态帖自然触达仅约粉丝的 0.5%，Reels 约 9%；平台内部的格式与场景差异，比粉丝量更能决定触达。
发布 2026.11.11 期号 2026-11 篇幅 约 1,800 字 数据来源 3 份公开基准

核心结论 Key Takeaways

  1. Instagram 帖子平均自然触达约粉丝量的 3.5%，同比下降 12%；Facebook 主页约 1.25%，2012 年为 16%。
  2. 同一账号内，静态图片帖触达约 0.5%，Reels 约 9%，相差 18 倍——格式比粉丝量更能决定触达。
  3. TikTok 商业账号自然触达约 5.7%，采用波浪式分发，完播率决定能否进入下一波。
  4. 四个场景排序信号不同：Feed 看兴趣、Reels 看时长、Stories 看互动历史、Explore 看收藏。

01先修正一个前提：粉丝量不再是触达的上限

很多品牌仍然默认「粉丝越多，看到的人越多」。对 872,075 条品牌帖的基准研究发现：2025 年 Instagram 帖子的平均自然触达约为粉丝量的 3.5%，同比再降 12%；Facebook 全部主页平均约 1.25%，而 2012 年这一数字是 16%。更值得注意的是同平台内的分化——小型主页触达 4.35%，大主页只有 0.50%，相差 8 倍以上。

换句话说，平台不是在按粉丝分发，而是在按信号分发。粉丝量只决定「有多少人可能看到」，实际触达由内容质量信号重新分配。

02同一平台内部：格式决定触达

同一账号、同一条时间线里，Instagram 静态图片帖的自然触达约 0.5%，Reels 约 9%——18 倍的差距完全由格式决定。这意味着在资源有限时，把静态图文预算转向短视频，往往比追加投放金额更能改变结果。

TikTok 的分发机制更直接：新内容先推给小规模测试人群，完播率、评论与分享高于平均，才进入下一波更大的分发。前 3 秒能否给出价值，几乎决定了这条内容的命运。

03四个场景，四套排序逻辑

场景核心排序信号内容含义
Feed 信息流兴趣图谱匹配（Instagram 超半数内容来自未关注账号，Facebook 约 54%）关系维护与话题延展，不再保证粉丝可见
Reels / 短视频观看时长、完播率、分享前 3 秒钩子、单一信息点、可被转发的情绪或实用价值
Stories互动历史（浏览、回复、投票）维持性触达与私域转化，不适合拉新
Explore 探索页收藏权重显著高于点赞工具型、清单型、可保存的实用内容

这张表最常见的误用，是用一套 Brief 通投四个场景：Feed 的内容被塞进 Reels，缺钩子；Stories 的内容被做成静态图，缺互动引导。结果不是内容不好，而是内容去了不该去的地方。

04对红人合作的实际含义

  • 选号看形态匹配：一个 5 万粉、擅长 Reels 的创作者，单条触达可能超过一个百万粉但只发静态图的账号。
  • Brief 按场景拆：Feed 讲关系与场景、Reels 讲前 3 秒与完播结构、Stories 讲互动引导，一份 Brief 只服务一个场景。
  • 预算随格式调：同预算下优先保障短视频产能，再考虑追加投放。
算法奖励的是「停留、完播、收藏、分享」这类质量信号，而不是发布行为本身。对准这些信号，比追逐任何单次算法更新都更有效。

Key Takeaways

  1. The average Instagram post reaches about 3.5% of followers, down 12% year over year; Facebook pages average 1.25%, versus 16% in 2012.
  2. On the same account, a static photo reaches about 0.5% while Reels reach roughly 9% — an 18x gap decided by format.
  3. TikTok business accounts reach about 5.7% organically through wave-based distribution, where completion rate unlocks the next wave.
  4. Each surface ranks differently: Feed by interest, Reels by watch time, Stories by interaction history, Explore by saves.

01Fix the premise: followers no longer cap your reach

Many brands still assume more followers mean more viewers. A benchmark study of 872,075 brand posts found the average Instagram post in 2025 reached only about 3.5% of its followers, down 12% year over year; Facebook pages average roughly 1.25%, versus 16% in 2012. The split inside each platform is sharper: small Facebook pages reach 4.35% of followers while large pages reach 0.50% — an eightfold gap.

Platforms distribute by signal, not by follower count. Follower count only defines how many people could see a post; actual reach is reallocated by content quality signals.

02Inside one platform, format decides reach

On the same account and timeline, a static Instagram photo reaches about 0.5% of its audience while Reels reach roughly 9% — an 18x difference decided purely by format. When resources are limited, shifting static budget into short video often changes results more than adding spend.

TikTok's mechanism is more direct: a new post first goes to a small test group, and only above-average completion, comments and shares unlock the next, larger wave. The first three seconds largely decide a post's fate.

03Four surfaces, four ranking logics

SurfaceCore ranking signalContent implication
FeedInterest-graph matching (over half of Instagram feed content comes from unfollowed accounts; ~54% on Facebook)Relationship maintenance and topic extension; follower visibility is no longer guaranteed
Reels / short videoWatch time, completion rate, sharesFirst-3-second hook, single message, emotionally or practically shareable
StoriesInteraction history (views, replies, polls)Retention and DM conversion, not acquisition
ExploreSaves weighted far above likesUtility, checklists, save-worthy practical content

The most common mistake is one brief for all four surfaces: Feed content pushed into Reels without a hook; Stories built as static images without interaction prompts. The content isn't bad — it is simply in the wrong place.

04What this means for creator partnerships

  • Select by format fit: a 50K-follower Reels specialist can out-reach a million-follower account posting only static photos.
  • Split briefs by surface: Feed for context and relationship, Reels for hooks and completion structure, Stories for interaction prompts — one brief per surface.
  • Move budget with format: secure short-video capacity first, then consider paid amplification.
Algorithms reward dwell, completion, saves and shares — quality signals, not posting itself. Aligning with these signals beats chasing any single algorithm update.
